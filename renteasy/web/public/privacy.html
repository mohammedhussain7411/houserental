Privacy Policy for RentEasy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

RentEasy ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a landlord-centric property management and rent collection application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information created and stored within RentEasy.

1. Information Handled by RentEasy

RentEasy allows landlords to manage rental properties. Information entered by the user includes:

  • Property & Unit Details: Property names, addresses, unit numbers, and rent configurations.
  • Tenant Records: Tenant names, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, agreement dates, security deposits, and Aadhaar identification numbers (stored locally and securely under user discretion).
  • Financial Transactions: Monthly rent dues, arrears, payment receipts, payment modes (UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer), and timestamps.
  • Landlord Profile: Landlord name, email address, phone number, and UPI ID for digital receipt branding.

2. Storage and Data Ownership

Your data belongs 100% to you. RentEasy utilizes local client-side database storage and optional user-controlled Google Drive cloud backups. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest any tenant or landlord financial information to third parties.

3. Cloud Backup via Google Drive

When you use the "Save to Google Drive" feature, your encrypted backup bundle is transferred directly to your own personal Google Drive account. RentEasy does not access any other private files inside your Google Drive.

4. Messaging & Reminders (WhatsApp & SMS)

RentEasy provides direct communication tools to dispatch rent reminders and digital receipts via WhatsApp and SMS. Reminders are generated with tenant details you provide and are dispatched via standard secure mobile messaging channels.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement cryptographic verification hashes on all generated receipts to prevent tampering and protect landlord and tenant integrity.

6. Children's Privacy

RentEasy is intended for adult property managers and landlords. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
